How to measure
  1. Rest your fingertip lightly over the main back lens and the flash. Don't press hard, which squeezes the blood out.
  2. Keep your hand still and supported (e.g. phone flat on a table).
  3. Wait for “Good signal”, then tap Record.

The flash gets warm on long recordings. This is a research and demo tool, not a medical device.
